SC Helper Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

SC Helper is a browser extension for office staff. It runs entirely inside your browser.

What SC Helper handles

When you use the tools, SC Helper reads information shown on the web page you have open, such as student names, the last 4 digits of a Social Security number, payment plan amounts, dates and federal loan balances. It does this only after you click the extension button.

Where it goes

This information is kept in your browser's session memory only, so you can fill in and download your documents. It is erased when you close the browser, or sooner if you press "Erase now" in Settings. It is never written to disk, never sent to the developer, and never sent to any third party. SC Helper makes no network requests and has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What is saved on your computer

Only your own settings: school name, the blank form file you choose to load, light or dark mode, your pay-date calendar, and which version notes you have seen. These stay in your browser profile.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share data, use it for advertising, or use it to decide credit or lending. We do not collect it at all, because it never leaves your device.

Permissions

activeTab and scripting let the extension read the page you are viewing when you ask it to. storage keeps your settings and session memory. A content script on cod.ed.gov only removes dashes from a Social Security number you paste into a field there.

Independence

SC Helper is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any school, software vendor or government agency, including the U.S. Department of Education.
